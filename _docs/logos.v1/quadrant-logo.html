07 — Open Decisions

Mark orientation. Shown pointing up — stable, compass-like. Alternative: 90° clockwise (pointing right) for directional energy. The boat version points toward the bow, which argues for "pointing right" in a Western reading layout.

Outline vs. filled. Outline is faithful to the original and reads as more refined. Filled is more legible at small sizes and on screen. Recommendation: outline as primary, filled as the favicon/avatar variant only.

Wordmark case. Tracked caps (Option A) reads more institutional and precise — right for the brief. Title case reads warmer. Given the sole-practitioner context, title case may ultimately feel less cold. Worth testing both in email signature context.

Brass accent. Adds heritage and warmth, echoes naval/marine tradition. Optional — the two-color navy/white system is complete on its own. Brass could come in on the website as a hover state or rule color.

Stroke weight of inner line. Currently equal to outer lines. Reducing the bisector to 60–70% weight would create subtle hierarchy (outer = structure, inner = detail). Small change, worth testing.
