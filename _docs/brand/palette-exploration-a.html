Boatyard, without the AI beige

The usual AI look is a cream or parchment background, a terracotta or rust accent, soft warm greys, and every surface low in contrast. The original A did all of that. Each variant below keeps the wooden boat and changes one of those defaults.

The mark, wordmark (Jost) and body type (Inter) don't change, so only color differs between them.

Original A, for reference. The colors are each fine, but together they're cream paper, rust accent and brown-grey text, which matches what AI tools generate by default.

A1Brightwork

"Brightwork" is a boat's varnished wood. Here the dark mahogany is the main surface, with amber varnish as the highlight, like a cabin interior by lamplight. Light mode switches to near-white instead of cream.

Breaks the default by: designing dark-first and dropping cream entirely · Feels: rich, evening, premium · Risk: dark-first marketing sites are harder to make friendly; long text pages need the light mode

Color alone won't fix "generic"

AI-default design also comes from other choices. These go into the guide whichever palette wins:

  • Type: avoid the default pairing of a soft serif headline with grey sans body text. Jost's geometric tracked capitals already help.
  • Specific motifs: the fan-sector mark, a boot stripe, planking lines and chart soundings belong to Quadrant. Generic gradients, glows and sparkles don't.
  • Copy: no "elevate", "seamless", "unlock". The quiet-craftsman voice does a lot here.
  • Contrast: committed darks and clean whites rather than everything at 80% opacity on beige.
