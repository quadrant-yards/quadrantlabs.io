Slack Water, with more life in it

Original B is muted for three reasons. Its main green is dark and greyed (33% saturation at 25% lightness, close to charcoal). The background is a grey-green instead of a clean light. And I darkened the orange to pass contrast checks, which also dulled it.

Each variant fixes this differently. All of them use the rule from the Livery round: vivid colors fill solid areas (buttons, pills, bands) with a text color chosen for contrast, and a deeper shade is used only when the color is text.

Original B, for reference. Calm, but every color sits in the same narrow, greyed range.

B1Clear Day

The same Bay on a bright, windy afternoon. The water is a clear, saturated green-teal instead of grey, the background is a clean light, the marsh is summer green, and the buoy is full signal orange.

Changes: more saturation everywhere, same structure as B · Feels: fresh, confident, outdoors · Risk: teal plus orange is a familiar pairing; the marsh green and buoy-only use of orange keep it specific

B2Blue Crab

The Chesapeake's best-known resident has an olive-green shell, vivid cerulean claws and red-orange claw tips. The claw blue becomes the main color, the olive shell is the secondary, and the red tip is a small accent.

Changes: swaps the teal for a vivid but natural blue and adds a warm red · Feels: lively, regional, a little playful · Risk: blue is the most common brand color in tech; this cerulean has more green than standard UI blue, and the olive and red keep it from looking generic

B3Day Marker

Keeps the calm of the original but uses the buoy orange boldly and in quantity: primary buttons, a solid band, status pills. The impact comes from one loud color against a quiet background, like a channel marker on a still grey morning.

Changes: keeps B's neutrals; orange goes from a small accent to the main action color · Feels: calm with a strong signal · Risk: a lot of orange can feel like a warning; it works only if orange always means "act here"

How to compare

B1 is the same palette with more energy. B2 adds a new color story. B3 keeps the calm and adds one loud signal. They can mix: B1's clearer water with B3's bold orange buttons, for example.

Every text pair meets WCAG AA (4.5:1 or better), including dark text on the orange fills.
