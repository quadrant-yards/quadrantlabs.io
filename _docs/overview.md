I coach startup CTOs, CPOs and CEOs on their engineering, product, design and data teams. Sometimes I pinch-hit as the leader of those teams until I can help get the right team in place.

Companies work with me when:
1. they want to move faster
    * not moving as fast as the early days
    * not doing enough with AI / LLMs
    * not seeing the impact of product being shipped
2. they need help figuring out EPD hiring
    * need a VP of Eng but not sure what flavor (also Head of Product, Data or Platform)
    * have a potential leader who needs help stepping up into a leadership role
    * stuck in a "growth plateau"
3. they want stability through a transition in EPD
    * transitioning leadership
    * pivoting EPD strategy, still figuring out leadership

I approach each company similary:
1. diagnose (and stabilize, if necessary)
    * research and analyze how the team works today
    * write diagnosis: state of the world, options, recommendations
    * usually takes 2-4 weeks at 1-3 days / week, depending
2. decide and do (varies by company need. for example:)
    * manage the existing engineers, product managers, and eng managers / directors
    * design and sit on interview loops for directors / VPs of eng, prod, data
    * run planning and other product ceremonies
    * set the product roadmap
    * establish product analytics / top-level KPIs
    * reset collaboration with sales, marketing, and other partner teams

3. downshift (over time)
    * once the people, process and tech are in place, step back into coaching role
